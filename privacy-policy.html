Privacy Policy for Sweet Saga

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Developer: Shahid Ansari

This Privacy Policy explains how Sweet Saga ("the App", "we", or "us") handles user information when you play our mobile game on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

Sweet Saga works completely offline and respects your privacy. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location on external servers.

2. Local Game Data Storage

Your gameplay progress—including unlocked levels, star ratings, high scores, lives, boosters, and daily reward streaks—is stored locally on your device. Optional manual backup sync codes remain solely under your control.

3. Children's Privacy

Sweet Saga is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age.

4. Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all App data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling Sweet Saga.

5. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact: shahid.sweetsaga.support@gmail.com
